FAQs

Questions, answered

Does AI Conductor replace our statisticians, programmers or medical writers?

No. It drafts and runs checks. Your experts accept, revise or reject every output.

Do we need an existing protocol to start?

No. You can start from a blank slate, early drafts or past trials.

What happens when the protocol changes?

Changes are reflected across dependent documents, and your team reviews and approves them, with no manual re-sync across teams.

How does it catch data collection gaps?

It compares your CRF requirements against incoming EDC data automatically, so mismatches surface early instead of in manual reconciliation.

How do we trace an output back to its source?

Outputs are grounded in your source library, with versions, review history and an audit trail from raw data to the CSR.

How is AI-generated code validated?

Independent AI QC compares results across different AI models, then an expert approves.

Does it follow our templates and standards?

Templates and code libraries are configurable with your own, and outputs follow CDISC standards (SDTM and ADaM).

Can we start with one workflow?

Yes. Start with the workflow that matters now and expand to the full study lifecycle.

What systems does it connect with?

It is built to fit alongside Veeva, Medidata, AWS, Microsoft Azure and your own environment.

Can we test our study design before enrolling?

Yes. PhaseV’s simulation and optimization tools let teams see how a design performs across plausible scenarios before the first patient enrolls, and AI Conductor can connect to them. ✓?
