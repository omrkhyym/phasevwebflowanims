See BonV in action

Ask in plain language, such as exploring Phase 3 design options for a new study, and BonV interprets your intent and orchestrates the right capabilities, from target trial emulation and protocol design to site identification, heterogeneity analysis, and causal modeling. Built on PhaseV’s validated causal AI, BonV recommends and explains while your experts review and approve each step, so every result is transparent, traceable, and yours to decide.

Good tools still slow you down when no one can find the right one

Large sponsors already own powerful applications, datasets and methods. Adoption stalls at the handoffs: which tool, which data, which colleague. BonV is designed to replace that search with a conversation, while keeping decisions with your team.
