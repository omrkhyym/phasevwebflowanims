FAQs

Questions, answered

How do I choose the right clinical trial sites?

Match each site’s patients to your protocol’s eligibility criteria, then rank sites on protocol fit, recruitment potential, and operational performance. ClinOps Optimizer does this and shows the reasons behind each ranking.

How can I reduce enrollment delays?

Test your eligibility criteria early, choose sites that can reach the right patients, and track enrollment against a forecast. ClinOps Optimizer supports all three.

How is ClinOps Optimizer different from my CRO’s recommendations?

It works alongside your CRO. PhaseV draws on data from many CROs and studies, so it can surface sites your CRO has not worked with, and it explains why sites succeed.

How does it handle new sites or sites with little data?

It borrows from related data, such as a high-performing investigator’s past enrollment at a previous institution, and shows the added uncertainty in the forecast.

Where does the data come from, and is it protected?

Public sources, licensed real-world data, partnerships, and sponsor data shared under your confidentiality terms. PhaseV works only with de-identified or anonymized data.

What do I need to start, and how long does it take?

A protocol, an indication, and target geographies are enough. Historical trial data and site lists make the results more specific. If your indication is already live on our self-serve platform, you can have results in 48 hours. Otherwise, expect 2-3 weeks.
