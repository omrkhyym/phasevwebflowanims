FAQs

Questions, answered

How is this different from a standard subgroup analysis?

Post hoc subgroup searches are prone to false positives. Response Optimizer combines multiple CATE estimators, tests stability across methods and thresholds, runs false discovery analysis, and checks findings against external evidence before calling anything a responder profile.

Can you help after a failed or ambiguous trial?

Yes. We determine whether benefit was truly absent, diluted by predictable variation, or concentrated in a reproducible population. If there is no stable subgroup, we say so and support a clear no-go.

Can we engage before unblinding?

Yes. Before unblinding, we can validate and lock a single prognostic score for prespecified covariate adjustment, improving precision without changing the estimand or trial operations.

What data do you need, and how long does it take?

SDTM, ADaM, or CSV study data, plus the protocol, SAP, and study context. The timeline for Response Optimizer timeline is approximately 4 weeks.

Will the findings stand up to regulatory scrutiny?

Every engagement delivers a regulatory-discussion-ready evidence package with definitions, estimates, intervals, assumptions, and limitations visible. Prognostic scores can be prespecified where required. We show what the evidence supports and don’t promise a subgroup.

What can we do with the results?

Validated findings can inform analysis adjustment, enrichment, stratification, biomarker strategy, I/E criteria, or next-study design, with the potential impact on sample size, timeline, and probability of success quantified.
