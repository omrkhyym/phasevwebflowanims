The Trial Optimizer workflow

A simulation workbench
for design trade-off exploration

Set Up

Set up a trial design in minutes.

Start from a prior design or go through a guided setup.

Use the GenAI Assistant to retrieve relevant public studies and inform placebo or standard-of-care response, with source-level review and selectable reference trials.

Explore

Explore how clinical, operational, and statistical assumptions and decisions interact.

Vary treatment and control effects, recruitment, allocation, interim strategy, and other consequential assumptions.

Compare a wide range of designs, including fixed, adaptive, Bayesian, and your own customized designs, to find the one that suits your study best.

Simulate

Simulate how each design performs.

Configure and run rapid Monte Carlo simulations across the selected scenarios and design options.

Examine and compare simulated outcomes side by side.

Optimize

Refine the selected design.

Choose what outcomes to optimize for - minimizing sample size and/or trial duration under null and alternative hypotheses - and what parameters to optimize over - e.g. when to have interim looks and stopping criteria.

PhaseV’s proprietary stochastic optimization algorithm searches the parameter space for further improvement.

Export the selected design with supporting evidence, audit trail, and reproducibility settings.

FAQs

Questions, answered.

What is Trial Optimizer?

Trial Optimizer is a simulation workbench for clinical trial design. It lets you set up candidate designs, vary the assumptions that drive them, simulate performance across scenarios, and refine the design you select.

Which designs can I compare?

Fixed, adaptive, and Bayesian designs, along with your own customized designs. You can compare them side by side across the same set of scenarios.

How does it handle uncertainty in my assumptions?

You vary treatment and control effects, recruitment, allocation, interim strategy, and other assumptions, then simulate each design across those scenarios. The results show how each design performs when assumptions miss, so you can see which designs hold up.

What does the GenAI Assistant do?

It retrieves relevant public studies to inform placebo or standard-of-care response. You review each source and select the reference trials you want to use.

How does the optimization work?

After you select a design, PhaseV’s proprietary stochastic optimization algorithm searches the parameter space for further improvement against your objectives and constraints.

What can I export?

The selected design, with supporting evidence, an audit trail, and reproducibility settings.

Does Trial Optimizer forecast enrollment?

You can vary recruitment assumptions and see their effect on design performance. For forecasts built from site-level and regional data, use ClinOps Optimizer.

Do I need the rest of the PhaseV platform?

No. Trial Optimizer is complete on its own. When connected to the broader platform, reviewed insights can enter the scenario space and approved parameters can return to connected documents for expert review.

How long does setup take?

You can set up a design in minutes, starting from a prior design or a guided setup.
